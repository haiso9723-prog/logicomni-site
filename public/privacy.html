Privacy information

Simple pages.
Clear boundaries.

Effective 27 September 2026. This notice explains the information involved when you visit this site or choose to email the team.

01

Who to contact

The LogicOmni team is the contact for this independent research project. For a privacy question about this site or an email you sent, write to contact@logicomni.com.

02

The website

These are static pages with no account, form, analytics, advertising or remote font. The site code sets no cookies. It does not ask you to submit personal information to the website. Its local visual effects use pointer position and screen size only in your browser's memory; they do not store or transmit those values.

03

Hosting

A hosting provider may process technical request data, such as IP address, time, requested page and browser details, to deliver and protect the site. The provider's own handling of those technical records may depend on its service settings and policies.

04

Optional email

Choosing an email link opens your email app; the website sends nothing automatically. If you decide to send an email, your address and message pass through your email provider and the project's email provider, Zoho Mail. The team may use a general message only to answer that inquiry. Sending a general email is not consent to unrelated contact or research participation.

On the research-participation page, sending the clearly labelled opt-in email allows the team to reply only to assess fit and arrange the described conversation. It is not consent to unrelated marketing, a mailing list or another research topic. You may withdraw before or during the conversation by replying to the same thread.

Please avoid confidential, sensitive, customer or employer-controlled information. To ask about an email you sent, including a deletion request, use contact@logicomni.com. Handling may depend on applicable obligations and the providers involved; no fixed retention period or processing location is claimed here.
