Voluntary research participation

Help us understand
one bid-risk process.

The LogicOmni team is seeking estimators and operations professionals at small US specialty contractors for an email-based conversation about how issued addenda are found, reviewed and reflected in a bid before submission. Participation is voluntary and is not a sales commitment.

Who this is for

A focused
research fit.

You may be a fit if all of these points describe you.

You work at a US electrical, fire-protection, glazing, roofing or other specialty contracting company with approximately 5–100 employees.

Your role touches estimating, preconstruction, project management, operations or ownership, and you understand how late addenda are checked against the current estimate and bid package.

You can discuss the workflow without sharing drawings, bid files, prices, customer names, controlled information or other confidential material.

01

What we will discuss

One asynchronous email thread covering up to eight questions about where addenda arrive, who reviews them, how trade impact is recorded, and what must be checked before submission. We will ask about the process, not request project documents or customer data.

02

Optional pilot concept

Research participation does not buy anything or create a subscription. If you separately request pilot information, we may send a public sample and the current validation concept for a month-to-month, email-delivered addendum impact brief. The pricing hypothesis is $149 per month for up to eight addenda, with no setup fee; the first three customers may later be offered $99 per month for their first three months only. This is not an available subscription or payment request. No payment or commitment occurs through this page, and any future offer would require separate written terms and readiness checks.

03

Your choice and withdrawal

You may stop at any time by replying to the same email thread. We will not add you to a mailing list. Permission to discuss the research process is separate from permission to receive pilot information, and either permission may be withdrawn.
