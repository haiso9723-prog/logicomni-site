Research approach

A deliberate path from question to decision.

The approach brings evidence, assumptions, constraints and remaining unknowns into one reviewable view.

Five deliberate stages

  1. 01

    Frame

    Define the question, affected people, intended outcome, exclusions and smallest useful learning boundary.

  2. 02

    Examine

    Separate supporting evidence, contradictory signals, assumptions and unknowns.

  3. 03

    Test carefully

    Choose a proportionate, consent-aware learning step and request only the information it needs.

  4. 04

    Review

    Present findings, limits and open questions in a form another person can challenge.

  5. 05

    Decide

    Continue, revise, pause or stop with evidence, risk, cost and reversibility visible.

Reviewable outputs

Useful artifacts, not a black box

Outputs are designed to make the reasoning inspectable: a problem brief, an evidence view, explicit uncertainties and a bounded next-step recommendation.

The exact scope depends on the question. This approach is not a guarantee of outcomes or a claim that paid services are currently available.
